🚀 Excited to announce our upcoming event "**Mastering Site Reliability Engineering**"

Join the DevOps Moldova Community for an engaging and knowledge-packed event "**Mastering Site Reliability Engineering**" dedicated to all local DevOps engineers, SREs, and technology enthusiasts passionate about building reliable, scalable, and high-performing systems.

📅 **Date**: 25 Oct 2025  
🕒 **Time**: 11:00 AM  
📍 **Location**: Noction Office  
bd. Stefan cel Mare si Sfant 182,  
Chisinau, 2004, Moldova  
🎟️ **Registration**: https://unde.io/events/1417

In today’s fast-paced cloud-native world, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) has become the cornerstone of modern operations. This event will dive deep into the principles, best practices, and real-world challenges of SRE — from incident management and service-level objectives to automation, observability, and cultural transformation within DevOps teams.

💡 What to Expect:

* Inspiring talks and technical sessions from local and international experts
* Practical case studies and demos on reliability and system performance
* Engaging networking opportunities with fellow DevOps professionals
* Insights into tools, frameworks, and workflows that make reliability measurable and actionable

🎯 Who Should Attend:

* DevOps Engineers
* Site Reliability Engineers (SREs)
* System Administrators
* Cloud Architects
* Developers interested in operational excellence and reliability

## Topics

### SRE and Chaos engineering
> Presenter **Ion Munteanu**,  Platform Engineer / DevOps Consultant

Ion Munteanu is a technical leader with over a decade of experience designing and scaling large-scale platforms. He has spent much of his career in the sports betting industry, driving infrastructure and reliability at companies like Caesars Sportsbook and William Hill, and has also worked on cutting-edge blockchain technologies.

Ion is passionate about solving complex problems and turning them into scalable, reliable solutions. Whether leading initiatives, mentoring engineers, or diving hands-on into architecture, he brings a high sense of ownership and a track record of building systems that perform under pressure.
